
This pack creates a safe plan for a future improved recorded-data paper rerun.

- `reports\paper_trading\paper_improvement_execution_sprint_close_pack\paper_improvement_execution_sprint_close_pack.json`

`reports\paper_trading\improved_recorded_data_paper_rerun_planning_pack`

Safety:

- NEUTRAL = no trade.
- No option selling.
- No futures/equity execution.
- No broker orders.
- No live market data.
- No real money.
- Strategy logic is not changed.
- No backtest is executed by this pack.
- This is not a profitability claim.
