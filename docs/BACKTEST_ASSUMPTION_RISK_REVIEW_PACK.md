# Backtest Assumption Risk Review Pack

This pack reviews the assumptions and risks behind the recorded-data paper backtest result.

It does not approve live trading and does not claim profitability.

It highlights:

- deterministic option reference pricing
- absence of real option-chain fills
- absence of broker execution
- high trade frequency risk
- exit-rule distribution risk
- single recorded dataset scope
- drawdown reference risk
- paper result interpretation guard

Safety scope:

- Paper/simulation only.
- No broker connection.
- No live market data request.
- No real orders.
- No real money.
- No profitability proof.
- No live trading approval.

Shortcut:

.\scripts\paper_trading\hqe_backtest_assumption_risk_review_pack.bat
