# Hunter Quant Engine (HQE)

# Module Flow

---

# Overview

Hunter Quant Engine (HQE) processes market data through a sequence of independent detection modules.

Each module has a single responsibility.

Each module produces immutable market events that become the input for later stages.

No module makes trading decisions.

# Module Responsibilities

## Candle

Represents raw OHLCV market data.

---

## Swing Detection

Detects significant swing highs and swing lows.

Output:

- SwingPoint

---

## Market Structure

Builds the overall market structure using swing points.

Output:

- MarketStructure

---

## BOS

Detects Break of Structure events.

Output:

- BOSPoint

---

## CHOCH

Detects Change of Character events.

Output:

- CHOCHPoint

---

## Liquidity

Detects buy-side and sell-side liquidity.

Output:

- LiquidityPoint

---

## Equal Levels

Detects equal highs and equal lows.

Output:

- Equal High
- Equal Low

---

## Liquidity Clusters

Groups nearby liquidity into clusters.

Output:

- LiquidityCluster

---

## Liquidity Sweep

Detects liquidity sweeps.

Output:

- LiquiditySweep

---

## Fair Value Gap

Detects market imbalances.

Output:

- FairValueGap

---

## Order Block

Detects institutional order blocks.

Output:

- OrderBlock

---

## Strategy Engine

Consumes all market events.

Produces:

- Trade opportunities

No market detection happens here.

---

## Backtesting

Evaluates strategy performance using historical data.

---

## Paper Trading

Executes simulated trades without real capital.

---

## Live Trading

Connects strategies to brokers and exchanges.

---

## AI Decision Engine

Consumes every event generated by HQE.

Possible future uses:

- Trade filtering
- Probability estimation
- Risk optimization
- Position sizing
- Adaptive strategies

AI never replaces detection engines.

Detection remains deterministic.

---

# Design Rule

Data always flows forward.

Modules never modify previous events.

Every module is independent.

Every event is immutable.

Every responsibility belongs to exactly one module.
